Common Questions

When are fees charged?

Fees are deducted automatically from the vault at the time of a successful release. No upfront charges — you only pay when the deal closes.

What currencies are accepted?

We support major fiat currencies (USD, EUR, GBP) and selected digital assets. Contact us to confirm availability for your specific transaction type.

What happens if a dispute isn't resolved?

For Institutional and Enterprise clients, EscrowGuard's legal arbitration team reviews all submitted evidence and delivers a binding decision within 10 business days.

Is there a minimum transaction size?

The Secure Personal plan has a $50 minimum fee floor. The Institutional plan has a $250 floor. Enterprise clients negotiate custom minimums.
