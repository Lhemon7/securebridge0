Protocol Security

How we eliminate trust through mathematics and cryptography.

Built from the Ground Up

Every layer of EscrowGuard is engineered so that no single person — including us — can access your funds without authorization.

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

We never hold your private keys. Your signatures are generated locally on your device, ensuring that even if our servers are compromised, your funds remain completely inaccessible to any attacker.

Cold Storage Integration

For Elite Shield clients, fund addresses are generated using hardware-isolated environments (HSM), providing an air-gapped layer of physical security that keeps assets completely offline.

End-to-End Encryption

All communications between parties are encrypted using AES-256. Transaction details, participant identities, and vault contents are never transmitted in plain text across our infrastructure.

Immutable Audit Logs

Every action — every signature, approval, rejection, and time-stamp — is written to a tamper-proof audit trail. Generate compliance reports instantly for regulatory or internal requirements.

Time-Lock Delays

High-value transactions can be configured with mandatory delay windows — up to 72 hours — giving all stakeholders a final audit period before any signed release becomes irreversible.

Multi-Party Quorum

No single signer can authorize a release unilaterally. Configurable M-of-N quorum logic ensures collective authorization is always required, eliminating insider threats entirely.
