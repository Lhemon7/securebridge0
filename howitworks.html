The Multi-Sig Process

How we protect your high-value transactions from end to end.

01

Setup & Invite

The initiator creates a secure vault and defines the Quorum (e.g., 2-of-3 signatures). Participants receive encrypted invitations to join the transaction as buyers, sellers, or neutral observers.

02

Asset Funding

The buyer deposits the agreed-upon funds into the EscrowGuard Vault. Our system provides a cryptographic proof-of-funds to the seller, confirming the assets are secured and ready for release.

03

Verification Period

The seller performs their obligations — shipping goods, transferring domains, or providing services. The buyer has a pre-agreed window to inspect and verify the delivery.

04

Multi-Sig Release

Once both parties are satisfied, they provide their digital signatures. If a dispute arises, the EscrowGuard arbiter reviews the evidence and provides the final signature required to unlock the vault.

The Power of Quorum

In a traditional system, one person has all the power. In our system, no one person can move the money alone.
